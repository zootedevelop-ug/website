1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to Kabada — Lockbox Game (“Kabada”, “the game”, “we”, “our”), developed and provided by Zoote.

Kabada is a deduction-based number puzzle intended for players aged 13 and above. Privacy is part of the product design: the game does not need a user profile, cloud service, advertising network, analytics service, or internet connection to provide gameplay.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal or sensitive user data through Kabada. Game information used for normal play remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to Zoote.

No identity informationNo names, emails, phone numbers or dates of birth.
No location informationNo precise or approximate location.
No device trackingNo advertising or persistent device identifiers collected by the game.
No analyticsNo behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking.
No contacts or mediaNo contacts, camera, microphone, photos or videos.
No financial informationNo banking or payment-card information.

We do not sell personal data and do not share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics companies, or other third parties.

3. Information stored locally

Kabada may save non-personal gameplay information locally so your game can continue between sessions. This can include puzzle progress, completed-puzzle count, stars or ratings, attempts, hints, streaks, achievements, difficulty, code length, theme choices, and other game settings.

This information is used only to operate Kabada on your device. It is not uploaded to us or synchronized to an online account.

4. Device permissions and network access

Kabada is designed not to request special or sensitive device permissions for normal gameplay. It does not require access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone/SMS functions, or advertising identifiers.

The game is designed to operate completely offline and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

5. Advertising, analytics and third-party services

The current version of Kabada does not contain advertising, third-party analytics, social login, or cloud synchronization. We therefore do not use those services to collect or share information about players.

If a future version introduces a feature that changes these practices, this Privacy Policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated to accurately describe that version.

6. Players aged 13 and above

Kabada is intended for users aged 13 and above. The game does not ask users to provide their age, name, contact details, or other personal information, and it has no signup or sign-in feature.

Kabada does not knowingly collect personal information from children or teenagers. Parents and guardians with privacy questions may contact us below.

7. Security and data handling

Kabada minimizes privacy and security risk by avoiding collection and transmission of personal data. Locally stored game progress is handled through the application's local storage mechanisms and remains subject to the security protections of the user's device and operating system.

8. Data retention and deletion

Zoote does not retain Kabada player data on its servers because Kabada does not send player data to us.

Local game progress and settings may remain while Kabada is installed. Where the game provides a reset option, it can clear applicable statistics. Users can also remove locally stored data using their operating system's app-storage controls or by uninstalling Kabada. Device backup behavior, if enabled by the user or operating system, is controlled by the relevant platform settings rather than Kabada.

Because Kabada does not create user accounts, there is no Kabada online account to delete.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Kabada's features, legal requirements, or privacy practices change. The effective date will be revised. Material changes affecting data practices will also be reflected in relevant application and store disclosures.
